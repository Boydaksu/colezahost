## 1. Objectives & Scope
Execute and certify the complete concurrency, chaos, and provider uncertainty suite before V1 stable release:
1. **Double-Payment & Webhook Replay Idempotency:** Validate that concurrent or duplicated webhook delivery for identical transaction/payment records is absorbed without multiple payment records, balance corruption, or duplicate invoice settlement.
2. **Store Credit Competition & Negative Balance Prevention:** Validate that concurrent debit attempts against limited customer credit balances strictly respect database synchronization locks, ensuring balance never falls below zero.
3. **Server Capacity Contention:** Validate that concurrent order provisioning requests on saturated nodes strictly respect capacity boundaries (`max_accounts`, disk/bandwidth limits) and trigger deterministic, graceful rejection.
4. **Provider 429 Rate Limiting, Backoff & Jitter:** Validate exponential backoff progression, upper delay boundaries, bounded jitter variation, and hard cutoff at maximum attempts.
5. **Transient vs. Permanent Fault Classification:** Validate that transient network failures (429, 502, 503, 504, socket drops) qualify for automated retry, while permanent client/provider errors (401, 403, 404, 422, domain/username conflicts) immediately halt retry loops.
6. **Domain Registrar Socket Timeout Uncertainty & Reconciliation:** Validate that network drops during registration/renewal transition operations into `UNCERTAIN` state, which are subsequently resolved by background reconciliation without issuing duplicate registry commands.
7. **Missed Scheduler Catchup & Distributed Worker Collision:** Validate that distributed locks prevent multiple cron workers from simultaneously running catchup for missed windows, preventing duplicate renewal invoices or redundant service state transitions.
8. **Service Optimistic Concurrency Control:** Validate that concurrent modifications using stale version numbers trigger `ServiceConcurrencyException` and prevent intermediate corrupted states.
