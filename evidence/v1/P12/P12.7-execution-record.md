## 1. Subphase Scope & Objective
Implement ICANN-compliant domain expiration lifecycles, grace and redemption windows, restoration pricing calculations, and automated ERRP renewal reminders:
- **Lifecycle Policy & Metadata Calculation (`DomainLifecyclePolicy`, `DomainExpiryService`):**
  - Configurable grace and redemption periods (standard: 30-day grace, 30-day redemption).
  - Calculates detailed metadata: days until expiry, current lifecycle stage (`active`, `grace`, `redemption`, `cancelled`), grace end date, redemption end date, renewal eligibility, redemption restoration eligibility, standard renewal fee, and redemption restore fee.
- **Automated Lifecycle Progression (`evaluateAndAdvanceDomainLifecycles`):**
  - Evaluates registered domains against simulated or real reference dates.
  - Advances state machine transitions:
    - `active` -> `grace` upon expiration.
    - `grace` -> `redemption` when grace period lapses without renewal.
    - `redemption` -> `cancelled` when redemption period lapses without restoration.
  - Logs chronological timeline events for every stage transition (`domain_entered_grace`, `domain_entered_redemption`, `domain_lifecycle_cancelled`).
- **ICANN ERRP Renewal Reminders (`DomainRenewalReminder`):**
  - Storage table `domain_renewal_reminders` with a strict `UNIQUE (domain_id, reminder_type, expiry_date)` constraint for 100% duplicate protection.
  - Dispatches notices at ICANN-mandated ERRP intervals:
    - Pre-expiry notices: 30 days, 7 days, 1 day before expiration.
    - Post-expiry notices: 1 day, 5 days after expiration.
    - Redemption warning notices.
  - Automatically discovers registrant contact email and records `reminder_sent` timeline audit events.
