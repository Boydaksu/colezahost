## 1. Subphase Scope & Objective
Establish high-integrity, locale-aware CSV parsing and import pipelines feeding directly into the migration staging engine:
- **Locale-Aware Parsing Engine (`LocaleAwareCsvParser`, `CsvLocaleConfig`, `LocaleDateFormat`):**
  - Automatic delimiter detection across common formats: `,` (comma), `;` (semicolon - continental European/Excel default), `\t` (tab), `|` (pipe).
  - Transparent UTF-8 BOM (`\xEF\xBB\xBF`) stripping preventing first-column header corruption from Microsoft Excel exports.
  - Localized numeric parsing: handles European/TR dot thousands and comma decimals (`1.250,75 €`), standard US comma thousands and dot decimals (`$1,250.75`), negative parentheses accounting notations (`(350.50)`), and currency symbol stripping.
  - Localized date normalization: converts ISO (`2026-10-15`), DMY (`15/10/2026` or `15.10.2026`), and MDY (`10/15/2026`) into standardized ISO 8601 `YYYY-MM-DD` strings.
- **Mapping Profiles & Resilient Headers (`CsvMappingProfile`):**
  - Preconfigured profiles for `client`, `invoice`, `service`, and `domain` entities.
  - Multi-language and formatting tolerance: supports space-, hyphen-, and underscore-insensitive matching (e.g. `first_name`, `first name`, `firstname`, `ad`).
  - Distinguishes numeric and date columns requiring locale parsing vs literal strings.
- **CSV Import Service & Zero Silent Loss Pipeline (`CsvImportService`, `CsvImportResult`):**
  - Parses rows, maps columns, and ingests records directly into `StagingPipelineService`.
  - Malformed CSV syntax lines (column count mismatches) are automatically staged under `CSV-CORRUPTED-L{line}` and placed into `QUARANTINED` status with exact error diagnostics.
  - Strict audit verification: `unaccounted_diff === 0` and `isZeroSilentLossAchieved() === true`.
