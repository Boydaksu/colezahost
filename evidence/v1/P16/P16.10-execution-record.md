## 1. Subphase Scope & Objective
Establish a formal, verifiable operational cutover process, end-to-end Golden Dataset validation, and cryptographic audit seals:
- **Cutover Checklist & Validation Engine (`CutoverChecklistItem`, `CutoverChecklistReport`, `CutoverChecklistService`):**
  - Evaluates operational invariants before permitting production cutover:
    1. `source_read_only`: Strict read-only mode verified on source WHMCS connector.
    2. `staging_terminal_status`: 100% of staged records reached terminal status (`MIGRATED` or `QUARANTINED`). Zero silent data loss certified (`unaccounted_diff === 0`).
    3. `financial_reconciliation`: Multi-currency invoices, payments, and credit ledgers reconciled with 0.00 difference down to cent precision across USD, TRY, and EUR.
    4. `unsupported_data_accounting`: Zero dropped schema fields; all custom and unmapped source fields preserved in staging metadata.
    5. `migration_safety_hold`: Entity-level migration holds active, suppressing auto-suspensions and client emails.
    6. `execution_checkpoint`: Step checkpoint indicates pipeline completed with zero pending tasks.
  - Fail-fast enforcement: If any mandatory item is failed or incomplete, cutover seal generation is blocked with descriptive errors.
- **Cryptographic Cutover Audit Seal (`CutoverAuditSeal`):**
  - Generates immutable, tamper-evident audit certificate upon successful checklist clearance.
  - Binds batch ID, approval signatory, total migrated entity count, staging metrics, financial reconciliation summary, unsupported field statistics, and SHA-256 integrity hash.
  - Validates `verifyChecksum()` for audit trail and compliance defense.
- **Golden Migration E2E Simulation (`CutoverAndGoldenMigrationE2ETest`):**
  - Validates full end-to-end lifecycle on realistic, multi-currency WHMCS dataset:
    - 3 clients across international jurisdictions (USD, TRY, EUR)
    - Multiple hosting and server products
    - Services and domain adoptions
    - Multi-currency invoices and exact transaction payment allocations
    - Credit ledger entries
    - Support departments, tickets, and custom fields
    - Dry-run preflight check -> live execution with step checkpointing -> migration hold application -> notification suppression -> cutover checklist validation -> cryptographic audit seal.
