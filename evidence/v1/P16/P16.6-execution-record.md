## 1. Subphase Scope & Objective
Implement the migration engine for financial entities (invoices, invoice items, payment transactions, payment allocations, and credit balances) with exact source-to-target ledger zero-diff reconciliation and Zero Silent Data Loss certification:
- **WHMCS Financial Extractor (`WhmcsFinancialExtractor`):**
  - Read-only extraction across `tblinvoices`, `tblinvoiceitems`, `tblaccounts`, and `tblclients` (credits).
  - Currency resolution (`tblcurrencies` ID -> ISO-4217 code).
  - Aggregation of source invoice totals, payment totals, and credit totals grouped by currency.
- **WHMCS Financial Migrator (`WhmcsFinancialMigrator`):**
  - **Invoices & Items:**
    - Staged into staging records (`StagingRecordStatus::STAGED`).
    - Validated by `CanonicalValidationEngine` (checks client association, ISO currency, and mathematical consistency `subtotal + tax == total`).
    - Migrated into target `invoices` table preserving minor unit accuracy (`subtotal_minor`, `tax_total_minor`, `total_minor`).
    - Line items migrated into `invoice_items` table.
    - Staging records updated to `MIGRATED`.
  - **Payments & Allocations:**
    - Incoming payments (`amountin > 0` in `tblaccounts`) staged and validated.
    - Migrated into target `payments` table with `amount_minor`, `fee_minor`, `net_amount_minor`, and gateway references.
    - Invoices referenced in payments are mapped and linked via `payment_allocations`.
    - Corresponding target invoices updated with `paid_amount_minor`.
    - Staging records updated to `MIGRATED`.
  - **Client Credit Balances:**
    - Staged and deposited into Coleza `credit_ledger` as `deposit` entries with reason `"Migrated WHMCS credit balance"`.
    - Staging records updated to `MIGRATED`.
- **Multi-Currency Zero-Diff Financial Reconciliation (`WhmcsFinancialReconciliationReport`):**
  - Source ledger total vs Target ledger total compared for each currency:
    - Invoices diff: `round(source - target, 2) === 0.00`.
    - Payments diff: `round(source - target, 2) === 0.00`.
    - Credits diff: `round(source - target, 2) === 0.00`.
  - Financial reconciliation is certified only when diff is exactly `0.00` across all currencies.
- **Zero Silent Data Loss Certification:**
  - 100% terminal state invariant certified: `unaccountedDiff === 0` and `inFlightCount === 0`.
  - Unresolved client references and mathematically inconsistent invoices quarantined with exact diagnostics.
