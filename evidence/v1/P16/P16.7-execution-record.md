## 1. Subphase Scope & Objective
Implement the migration pipeline for WHMCS support tickets, replies, attachments, and custom fields, and establish the Constitution §11 & §12 Unsupported-Data Accounting Audit Engine:
- **WHMCS Support Extractor (`WhmcsSupportExtractor`):**
  - Read-only extraction of departments (`tblticketdepartments`), tickets (`tbltickets`), replies (`tblticketreplies`), attachments (`tblticketattachments`), and custom fields (`tblcustomfields` / `tblcustomfieldsvalues`).
  - Attachments indexed with filenames, tickets mapped with conversation message histories.
- **Support Migrator (`WhmcsSupportMigrator`):**
  - **Departments:**
    - WHMCS ticket departments migrated to Coleza `support_departments` (`name`, `description`, `email`, `is_public`, `sort_order`).
    - Department ID mapping recorded with automatic fallback to General Support.
  - **Tickets & Messages:**
    - Staged into staging records (`StagingRecordStatus::STAGED`).
    - Validated by `CanonicalValidationEngine`.
    - Ticket status and priority mapped to canonical values (`open`, `answered`, `in_progress`, `closed` / `low`, `medium`, `high`, `urgent`).
    - Created in `support_tickets` table with rich metadata preserving custom fields.
    - Initial opening message and all replies migrated into `support_ticket_messages` preserving client vs staff authorship flags (`is_staff`).
  - **Attachments:**
    - Files migrated into `support_ticket_attachments` with unique storage keys, content hashes (SHA-256), and timestamps.
- **Unsupported-Data Accounting Engine (`UnsupportedDataAccountant`, `UnsupportedDataReport`):**
  - Verifies that any unmapped source properties, legacy flags, or custom fields are preserved in target `metadata_json` under `unsupported_source_fields`.
  - Audits total mapped fields vs total preserved fields vs dropped fields.
  - Certifies Zero Silent Field Loss invariant: `totalDroppedFields === 0`.
- **Zero Silent Data Loss Certification:**
  - Certified terminal staging accounting: `unaccountedDiff === 0` and `inFlightCount === 0`.
  - Tickets referencing unmigrated clients are safely quarantined with exact diagnostics.
