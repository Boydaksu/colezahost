## 1. Subphase Scope & Objective
Establish robust migration hold protection and outbound communication suppression to protect imported data from inadvertent shockwaves, cron auto-suspensions, auto-terminations, renewals, and automated client emails:
- **Migration Hold State & Records (`MigrationHoldStatus`, `MigrationHoldRecord`, `MigrationHoldRepository`):**
  - Granular entity-level hold tags (`service`, `domain`, `invoice`, `client`) and platform-wide batch holds (`GLOBAL`).
  - Action-level suppression filtering (e.g. `['suspend', 'terminate']` or `['*']`).
  - Tracks hold metadata: `batch_id`, `held_at`, `released_at`, `released_by`, `release_notes`.
  - Persisted in `migration_holds` database table with multi-engine support.
- **Migration Hold Domain Service (`MigrationHoldService`):**
  - `holdEntity()` and bulk `holdEntities()` to place migrated accounts and financial records under protection.
  - `activateGlobalHold()` and `releaseGlobalHold()` for batch-level silence windows.
  - `isAutomationSuppressed($entityType, $entityId, $action)` verifying whether background cron tasks (e.g. suspension, termination, auto-renewal) should execute or pause.
  - Granular and bulk graduation/release (`releaseEntity()`, `releaseBatch()`).
  - Comprehensive status reporting (`getHeldEntitiesSummary()`).
- **Notification Suppression Manager (`SuppressedNotification`, `SuppressedNotificationRepository`, `NotificationSuppressionManager`):**
  - Intercepts all outbound automated emails/notifications targeted at entities under active hold or during global migration silence windows.
  - Zero silent loss: Suppressed notifications are not silently discarded; they are fully logged into `suppressed_notifications` table with recipient, template, payload data, and timestamp.
  - Safe administrative graduation & replay: `replaySuppressed()` enables administrators to inspect held notifications post-migration and dispatch approved communications.
  - `getSummary()` and `getSuppressedNotifications()` for audit visibility.
- **End-to-End Orchestrator Integration (`MigrationExecutionOrchestrator`):**
  - Integrated into `executeLive()` with option `'apply_migration_hold' => true`.
  - Automatically tags all migrated services, domains, invoices, and clients into `migration_holds`.
  - Automatically activates global hold during batch processing and safely graduates platform while keeping entity-level safety holds active for staff verification.
