## 1. Components Implemented
- `Coleza\Domain\Documents\DocumentType`: Business document varieties enum (`INVOICE`, `QUOTE`, `PROFORMA`, `RECEIPT`, `CREDIT_NOTE`, `CONTRACT`).
- `Coleza\Domain\Documents\Rendering\DocumentParty`: Value object representing commercial issuers and recipients with tax identification, contact, address formatting, and brand details.
- `Coleza\Domain\Documents\Rendering\DocumentItemLine`: Normalized line item model capturing description, quantity, unit price, tax rate, tax amounts, line discounts, and totals.
- `Coleza\Domain\Documents\Rendering\DocumentTaxBreakdown` & `DocumentTotals`: Financial tax summaries grouped by rate and multi-part totals (subtotal, discount, taxes, grand total, paid amounts, balance due).
- `Coleza\Domain\Documents\Rendering\DocumentViewModel`: Unified immutable document view model capturing metadata, currency, dates, parties, bank transfer coordinates, notes, terms, and QR/verification links.
- `Coleza\Domain\Documents\Rendering\DocumentLocalization`: Localization manager supporting Turkish (`tr`) and English (`en`) translations, recipient-locale-driven rendering, and locale-specific currency/date formatting.
- `Coleza\Domain\Documents\Rendering\DocumentTemplateEngine`: Semantic HTML5 compiler with print-ready CSS (`@page`, page-break isolation, high-contrast tables, status badges, XSS escaping, UTF-8 character support).
- `Coleza\Domain\Documents\Rendering\PdfRendererInterface` & `PurePhpPdfRenderer`: Shared-host compatible pure PHP PDF 1.4 binary engine generating compliant PDF streams without external headless daemons or exec dependencies.
- `Coleza\Domain\Documents\Rendering\DocumentRenderService`: Rendering coordinator converting domain entities (such as `Invoice`) into view-models, HTML, PDF binaries, and deterministic SHA256 integrity checksums.
