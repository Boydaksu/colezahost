## 1. Components Implemented
- `Coleza\Domain\Finance\Expenses\FinanceCategory`: Classification taxonomy model for income and expenses supporting tax deductibility flags, active state control, and uniqueness.
- `Coleza\Domain\Finance\Expenses\Vendor`: External supplier and vendor registry capturing corporate details, country codes, tax numbers, contact information, and notes.
- `Coleza\Domain\Finance\Expenses\Expense`: Expenditure entity tracking sequential identifiers (`EXP-YYYYMMDD-XXXXXX`), category and vendor bindings, currency, net and tax breakdowns, receipt metadata, and optional recurring links.
- `Coleza\Domain\Finance\Expenses\RecurringExpense`: Automated recurring vendor commitment tracker (e.g. server leases, control panel licenses) computing cyclical next due dates.
- `Coleza\Domain\Finance\Expenses\ExpenseService`: Operational expense manager coordinating:
  - Expenditure journaling linked directly to the `FinancialAccountService` transaction ledger for automated account debits.
  - Recurring expense batch scanner (`processDueRecurringExpenses`) generating expenditures on schedule and advancing recurring due dates.
  - Category, vendor, and expense search and listing queries.
