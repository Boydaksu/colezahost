## 1. Components Implemented
- `Coleza\Domain\Finance\Accounts\FinancialAccount`: Financial account model representing operational accounts across types (`bank`, `cash`, `gateway`, `other`), currency tracking, IBAN/account details, bank/branch names, active states, and single-default enforcement per currency and type.
- `Coleza\Domain\Finance\Accounts\AccountTransaction`: Immutable transaction journal entry recording sequential numbers (`TXN-YYYYMMDD-XXXXXX`), account bindings, entry types (`credit`, `debit`), amounts in minor units, derived post-transaction balance snapshots (`balance_after_minor`), sources (`payment`, `refund`, `expense`, `transfer`, `adjustment`), references, descriptions, and timestamps.
- `Coleza\Domain\Finance\Accounts\FinancialAccountService`: Core operational finance ledger manager providing:
  - Account provisioning and single-default constraint enforcement (`createAccount`, `getDefaultAccount`).
  - Real-time balance derivation directly from the immutable transaction ledger (`getBalance`).
  - Transaction journaling with strict currency validation and overdraft protection (`recordTransaction`).
  - Inter-account atomic transfers (`transferBetweenAccounts`) connecting source debits to destination credits.
  - Audited ledger views (`getLedgerView`) computing opening balances, total credit inflows, total debit outflows, closing balances, and chronological movement logs.
