## 1. Components Implemented
- `Coleza\Domain\Documents\Contracts\ContractStatus`: Contract lifecycle state enumeration (`DRAFT`, `PENDING_ACCEPTANCE`, `ACTIVE`, `TERMINATED`, `EXPIRED`).
- `Coleza\Domain\Documents\Contracts\ContractStateMachine`: State transition policy validator preventing illegal transitions once terminated or expired.
- `Coleza\Domain\Documents\Contracts\Contract`: Master Service Agreement (MSA) and Service Level Agreement (SLA) aggregate capturing commitment duration in months, uptime percentages (e.g. 99.95%), terms revisions, dual acceptance, and early termination liabilities.
- `Coleza\Domain\Documents\Contracts\ContractService`: Contract manager coordinating:
  - Sequential contract numbering (`CTR-YYYY-XXXXXX`) via central numbering.
  - Client digital signing capturing client IP address, user agent, timestamp, and typed legal signature.
  - Internal counter-signing and administrative review (`recordInternalAcceptance`) activating the contract.
  - Early contract termination with calendar-accurate calculation of remaining commitment liabilities and penalty fees (`calculateEarlyTerminationFee`).
  - Service-level queries linking contracts directly to provisioned services.
