## 1. Components Implemented
- `Coleza\Domain\Documents\Quotes\QuoteStatus`: Commercial quote lifecycle enumeration (`DRAFT`, `SENT`, `ACCEPTED`, `REJECTED`, `EXPIRED`, `CONVERTED`).
- `Coleza\Domain\Documents\Quotes\QuoteStateMachine`: Strict state transition regulator and illegal transition guard.
- `Coleza\Domain\Documents\Quotes\QuoteItem`: Granular line item representation for proposals with unit minor pricing, tax rates, billing cycle, and product mapping.
- `Coleza\Domain\Documents\Quotes\Quote`: Aggregate entity tracking parent revision linkage, sequential versioning, customer IP audit logs, acceptance/rejection timestamps, and converted order bindings.
- `Coleza\Domain\Documents\Quotes\QuoteService`: Full lifecycle proposal engine providing:
  - Initial proposal creation and revision generation (`createRevision`), automatically assigning revision identifiers (`QUO-YYYY-XXXXXX-R2`) and recording change rationale.
  - Quote dispatch (`sendQuote`), client acceptance logging client IP address (`acceptQuote`), and rejection recording (`rejectQuote`).
  - Automated quote expiry scanner (`expireQuotes`) invalidating past-due quotes.
  - Seamless commercial bridge (`convertToOrder`) translating accepted quotes into formal system Orders and billing invoices via `OrderService`.
