## 1. Components Implemented
- `Coleza\Domain\Documents\Proforma\ProformaStatus`: Proforma lifecycle state enumeration (`DRAFT`, `ISSUED`, `PAID`, `CONVERTED`, `CANCELLED`).
- `Coleza\Domain\Documents\Proforma\ProformaItem`: Proforma line item representation with quantity, unit minor pricing, tax rates, line subtotal, and tax breakdowns.
- `Coleza\Domain\Documents\Proforma\ProformaInvoice`: Dedicated non-fiscal advance payment document aggregate, tracking payment progress (`paidAmountMinor`, `balanceDueMinor`), conversion linkage to final tax invoices (`convertedInvoiceId`, `convertedInvoiceNumber`), and due dates.
- `Coleza\Domain\Documents\Proforma\ProformaService`: Domain service providing:
  - Sequential non-fiscal numbering (`PRO-YYYY-XXXXXX`) via the central numbering generator.
  - Proforma issuance and partial/full pre-payment tracking.
  - Conversion workflow (`convertToTaxInvoice`) generating an official fiscal tax invoice (`INV-YYYY-XXXXXX`) via `InvoiceService` upon settlement and permanently freezing proforma state as converted.
  - Explicit cancellation tracking with audit reason journaling.
