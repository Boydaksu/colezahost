## 1. Components Implemented
- `Coleza\Domain\Notifications\Classification\NotificationNature`: Nature classifier separating `TRANSACTIONAL` (non-opt-outable critical operational messages) from `MARKETING` (subject to customer consent and unsubscribe regulations).
- `Coleza\Domain\Notifications\Unsubscribe\UnsubscribeService`: Tamper-proof HMAC token generator and validator, plus RFC 8058 compliant `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` header generation.
- `Coleza\Domain\Notifications\Announcements\Announcement`: Public announcement and bulletin entity tracking publication status, categories (`maintenance`, `general`, `incident`, `promotion`), pinned alerts, and validity windows.
- `Coleza\Domain\Notifications\Announcements\AnnouncementService`: Announcement management coordinator providing:
  - Draft creation, publishing, and pinned maintenance alert queries.
  - Turkish character slug transliteration.
  - Marketing announcement email broadcasting (`broadcastMarketingAnnouncement`) filtering recipients against `NotificationCenterService` marketing opt-outs and automatically injecting one-click unsubscribe headers and links.
