## 1. Components Implemented
- `Coleza\Ui\Accessibility\AccessibilityValidator`:
  - Enforces WCAG 2.1 AA and `03-constitution/UI_CONSTITUTION.md` rules ("Accessibility from start: focus, labels/ARIA, contrast, reduced motion, no color-only status").
  - Automated HTML markup scanning:
    - Form inputs must have explicitly associated `<label for="...">` or `aria-label`/`aria-labelledby`.
    - Modal dialogs must specify `role="dialog"` and `aria-label` / `aria-labelledby`.
    - Images must contain `alt` descriptions.
    - Buttons must provide readable text content or accessible `aria-label`.
  - Color contrast ratio mathematical evaluator (`contrastRatio`) using relative luminance formulas to verify WCAG AA compliance (4.5:1 ratio baseline).
