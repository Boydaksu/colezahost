## 1. Components Implemented
- `Coleza\Domain\Provisioning\Retry\ProvisioningRetryPolicy`:
  - Exponential backoff calculation: `delay = min(maxDelay, baseDelay * (multiplier ^ (attempt - 1)) + jitter)`.
  - Rate-limit specific base delay adjustment (enforces minimum 60-second backoff for rate-limited requests).
  - Configurable randomized pseudo-jitter within bounded jitter factor to avoid thundering-herd synchronization.
  - Strict retry eligibility checks based on classification: transient network failures, rate limits, and recoverable resource exhaustion qualify, while hard validation, authentication, and conflict errors do not.
- `Coleza\Domain\Provisioning\Reconciliation\ReconciliationStatus`:
  - Status constants: `RECONCILED_EXISTS`, `RECONCILED_NOT_FOUND`, and `RECONCILED_UNREACHABLE`.
- `Coleza\Domain\Provisioning\Reconciliation\ReconciliationResult`:
  - Structured outcome DTO conveying probe findings, remote account summary, and resolution directive.
- `Coleza\Domain\Provisioning\Reconciliation\UncertainResponseReconciliationService`:
  - Actively probes remote hosting servers (via WHM `accountsummary`) before executing retry attempts or after network timeouts.
  - Ghost-creation resolution: if an account is confirmed active and healthy on the remote server despite a dropped connection or timeout, reconciles state directly into `active`, assigns placement, commits capacity, and marks the provisioning operation as completed without attempting a duplicate creation.
  - Verified non-existence: if the account does not exist remotely, safely clears the operation for retry without risk of duplicate domain/user collisions.
  - Unreachable handling: tracks unreachable nodes and postpones next attempt with exponential backoff.
- `Coleza\Domain\Provisioning\Services\ProvisioningRetryRunner`:
  - Batch worker processing due retry operations.
  - Pre-execution reconciliation probe to avoid duplicate remote executions.
  - Executes provisioning retries for verified clean states; advances retry counts and computes backoff timestamps.
  - Enforces permanent failure transition (`failed`) when maximum attempts are exhausted.
