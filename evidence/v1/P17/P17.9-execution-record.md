## 1. Scope and Objective
Implement automated rollback paths and strict privacy tombstone enforcement:
1. Provide `RollbackManager` to execute automated emergency rollbacks to verified pre-update backups.
2. Enforce Data Constitution Rule & Golden Scenario G08: Whenever a backup restore or rollback occurs, all historical Privacy Tombstones stored in out-of-band persistent storage (`FileTombstoneStore`) must be synchronized and re-applied against the operational relational database prior to opening production.
3. Guarantee that previously forgotten/erased users cannot have plaintext PII resurrected from old database snapshots.
