## 1. Subphase Scope & Objective
Implement enterprise governance and operational control primitives for the Coleza Automation Engine:
- **Approval Gate (Human-in-the-loop):** Safe execution interception requiring explicit human sign-off (with role checks, timeouts, approve/reject lifecycle, and execution resumption).
- **Execution Delay:** Time-based scheduling and delay queues (`delaySeconds`, `executeAt`, due execution polling, and dispatch/cancellation).
- **Observe Mode:** Passive/shadow monitoring mode evaluating live rule triggers and conditions without executing side-effects or state-mutating actions.
- **Dry-Run Mode:** Full simulation mode resolving parameter templates and projecting outcomes safely at runtime without executing live actions.
- **Rule Versioning & Diffing:** Immutable rule snapshots, version tracking (`versionNumber`, author, change log, diff comparisons).
- **Run History Repository:** Audited execution telemetry and repository (`AutomationRunRecord`) storing correlation IDs, duration, branch decisions, and output payloads.
