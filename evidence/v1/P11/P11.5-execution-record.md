## 1. Subphase Scope & Objective
Implement the enterprise lifecycle policy for overdue payments, grace periods, warning reminders, automated suspensions, terminations, and payment-driven unsuspensions:
- **Overdue Grace Policy:** Configurable grace period before suspension (`gracePeriodDays`, default: 7), termination grace period (`terminationGraceDays`, default: 30), customizable warning reminder days (e.g. [1, 3, 5]), manual approval hold option for terminations, and VIP/infrastructure tag exemptions.
- **Grace Warning Reminders:** Active overdue services within grace period receive timely warning notification emails without premature service interruption.
- **Automated Suspension:** Active services exceeding grace threshold are suspended through domain commands with explicit audit reasons and customer notification emails (`service_suspended`).
- **Automated Termination:** Suspended services exceeding termination grace threshold are permanently terminated through domain commands (`service_terminated`), with capacity reservation eviction and optional human approval gating.
- **Payment-Driven Auto-Reactivation & Cycle Advancement:** Settling invoices automatically reactivates suspended services, advances next due date for the renewed cycle, and dispatches customer reactivation notification emails (`service_unsuspended`).
