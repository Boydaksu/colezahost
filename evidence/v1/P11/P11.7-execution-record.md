## 1. Subphase Scope & Objective
Establish complete end-to-end certification of the hosting service lifecycle beyond first sale, and deliver production-grade missed-scheduler catchup mechanics for cron gaps or infrastructure downtime:
- **Missed-Scheduler Catchup:**
  - `MissedSchedulerCatchupService` detects execution gaps by persisting monotonic checkpoints in `scheduler_checkpoints`.
  - Accurately computes missing date sequences up to a bounded maximum window (`maxCatchupDays`), stepping through each historical day in sequential order.
  - Sequentially evaluates both renewal invoice generation (`ServiceRenewalScheduler`) and overdue grace/suspension policies (`OverdueLifecycleWorkflow`) for each missed day.
  - Guaranteed idempotency: `RenewalInvoiceService` unique period constraints prevent duplicate invoices during multi-day catchup runs.
  - Incremental checkpoint advances ensure uninterrupted recovery even in case of partial runs.
  - Detailed summary reporting in `CatchupBatchReport`.
- **Renewal Golden E2E Lifecycle Certification:**
  - Provisioning of recurring subscription service with monthly billing cycle.
  - Advance time to 14 days lead time: renewal scheduler executes, generates renewal invoice, dispatches invoice notification email.
  - Idempotency check: repeated runs do not duplicate invoices.
  - Advance time to 3 days overdue: overdue workflow issues warning reminder email while keeping service active.
  - Advance time to 7 days overdue (grace period expiration): overdue workflow automatically suspends service and sends suspension email.
  - Invoice payment settlement: client pays overdue invoice; `handleInvoicePaid` automatically unsuspends service, advances `next_due_date` by exactly 1 billing period, and dispatches reactivation notification.
  - Missed scheduler catchup: simulated 5-day scheduler outage; catchup coordinator recovers all missed days, advancing checkpoints and triggering second renewal cycle invoice on schedule.
  - Permanent termination: abandoned service remaining unpaid past termination grace threshold (30 days) is terminated, capacity reservation evicted, and notification dispatched.
