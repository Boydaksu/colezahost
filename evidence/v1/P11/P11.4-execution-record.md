## 1. Subphase Scope & Objective
Implement the service renewal scheduler and automated renewal invoice generation pipeline:
- **Renewal Policy:** Configurable advance lead days (`leadDays`, default: 14), policy-based auto-renew preference filtering (`autoRenewOnly`), invoice notification toggles, and optional custom invoice payment window offsets (`invoiceDueDays`).
- **Batch Evaluation & Scheduling:** Querying active candidate services due for renewal within lead days through `ServiceService::findServicesDueForRenewal`.
- **Strict Idempotency:** Guaranteed duplicate-prevention across repeat scheduler runs per service billing period (`renewal_records` table protection).
- **Invoice Generation & Customer Alerting:** Generating multi-item itemized renewal invoices with calculated tax snapshots and dispatching notification emails upon generation.
- **Batch Resilience:** Processing candidates independently so individual failures or validation errors are isolated and reported without halting the scheduler.
