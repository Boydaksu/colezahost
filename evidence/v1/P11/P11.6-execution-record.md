## 1. Subphase Scope & Objective
Implement safety mechanisms and circuit breakers protecting systems and client data against rogue automation rules, cascades, or uncontained loops:
- **Emergency Pause State & Manager:** Immutable state machine (`EmergencyPauseState`) and coordinator (`EmergencyPauseManagerInterface`, `EmergencyPauseManager`) allowing immediate global suspension (`ALL`), destructive action hold (`DESTRUCTIVE`), or domain-specific scoping (`BILLING`, `SERVICES`).
- **Destructive Action Registry:** Centralized catalog (`DestructiveActionRegistry`) maintaining recognized destructive commands (`service.terminate`, `service.cancel`, `service.suspend`, `server.destroy`, `account.purge`, `domain.delete`, `database.drop`) with configurable risk scoring.
- **Blast Radius Limiter:** Sliding-window rate limiter (`BlastRadiusLimiter`) that enforces a maximum ceiling on destructive operations within a rolling time frame (e.g. 5 destructive actions / hour). If the blast radius ceiling is breached, the limiter automatically trips the emergency pause (`DESTRUCTIVE` scope) to contain collateral damage.
- **Engine Safety Integration:** Integrated pause gates into `AutomationEngine`:
  - When global emergency pause is active, rule evaluation immediately halts with `RunStatus::PAUSED` and recorded audit justification.
  - When destructive pause is active, non-destructive actions proceed while destructive actions are blocked.
  - Successful destructive actions are tracked by the blast radius limiter; limit violations immediately reject execution and trigger containment pause.
- **Lifecycle Workflow Integration:** Integrated pause gates into `OverdueLifecycleWorkflow` so that overdue suspensions and terminations are suspended safely when emergency pause is tripped, preventing mass eviction during operational outages.
