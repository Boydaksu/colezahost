## 1. Subphase Scope & Objective
Deliver rapid response templating with canned responses and service desk broadcast announcements:
- **Canned Responses Engine:**
  - `CannedResponse` entity modeling response templates: `id`, `title`, `content`, `shortcut` (e.g. `/dns-refresh`), `department_id`, and active status.
  - Storage table `support_canned_responses`.
  - Placeholder template interpolation (`render(array $variables)`): dynamically replaces `{{ key }}` and `{{key}}` placeholders with contextual ticket and customer data.
  - Department scoping and shortcut lookup: matches shortcuts (`/greeting`), prioritizing department-specific templates over global templates.
- **Service Desk Announcements:**
  - `Announcement` model managing broadcast notices: `id`, `title`, `slug`, `content`, `type` (`general`, `maintenance`, `incident`, `info`), `is_public`, `is_active`, `is_pinned`, `published_at`, and `expires_at`.
  - Storage table `support_announcements` with unique slug index.
  - Publication lifecycle: `isLive` evaluates active state, publish threshold, and expiration window.
  - Priority feed: pinned announcements elevated above regular notices, ordered chronologically. Public vs authenticated portal segregation.
