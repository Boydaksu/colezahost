## 1. Subphase Scope & Objective
Deliver full ticket lifecycle, conversation threading, staff/customer replies, and private internal notes:
- **Ticket Entity & Generation:**
  - `Ticket` model managing support requests: `id`, human-friendly collision-resistant `ticket_number` (`TIC-YYYYMMDD-XXXXX`), `organization_id`, `user_id`, `department_id`, `assigned_to`, `status`, `priority`, `subject`, `service_id`, `domain_id`, `last_reply_at`, `last_reply_user_id`, `last_reply_by_staff`, `closed_at`, and timestamps.
  - Storage table `support_tickets` with unique ticket number index.
- **Conversation & Replies Stream:**
  - `TicketMessage` entity modeling messages and notes: `ticket_id`, `user_id`, `is_staff`, `is_internal_note`, `message`, metadata, and timestamps.
  - Storage table `support_ticket_messages` indexed by ticket.
  - Transactional ticket creation automatically inserts initial inquiry message into conversation.
- **Customer & Staff Replies:**
  - Staff replies update ticket status to `answered` (or custom valid status), record `last_reply_at`, `last_reply_user_id`, and set `last_reply_by_staff = true`.
  - Customer replies transition status to `customer_reply`, set `last_reply_by_staff = false`, and automatically reopen resolved or closed tickets (clearing `closed_at`).
- **Private Internal Notes:**
  - Staff internal notes are strictly tagged (`is_staff = 1`, `is_internal_note = 1`).
  - Filtering in `getConversation`: hidden from customer view (`includeInternalNotes = false`) and visible to staff view (`includeInternalNotes = true`).
  - Internal notes do not alter customer-visible last-reply status or timestamps.
- **Ticket Operations:**
  - Status updates validated via `TicketStatus::assertValidTransition`. Transition to `closed` records `closed_at`.
  - Priority updates validated via `TicketPriority::assertValid`.
  - Assignment and department transfers ensure assigned agents belong to the target department.
  - Flexible filtering by user, department, status/statuses, priority, and assigned agent.
