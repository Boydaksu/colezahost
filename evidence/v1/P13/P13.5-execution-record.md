## 1. Subphase Scope & Objective
Deliver SLA governance, department and priority targets, pause/resume tracking during holds, breach detection, and automated ticket priority escalation:
- **SLA Policy Engine:**
  - `SlaPolicy` entity modeling SLA targets: `id`, `name`, `department_id`, per-priority first-response and resolution thresholds (critical, high, medium, low in minutes), default flag, and active status.
  - Storage table `support_sla_policies`.
  - Resolution fallback logic: queries department-specific policy first, falls back to default active policy, then system built-in SLA standards.
- **Ticket SLA Lifecycle & Tracking:**
  - `TicketSla` model managing per-ticket timers: `ticket_id`, `policy_id`, `first_response_due_at`, `resolution_due_at`, `first_responded_at`, `is_first_response_breached`, `resolved_at`, `is_resolution_breached`, `status` (`active`, `met`, `breached`, `paused`), `paused_at`, and `total_paused_seconds`.
  - Storage table `support_ticket_sla` with unique ticket constraint.
- **First Response & Resolution Outcomes:**
  - `recordFirstResponse`: Identifies whether staff response occurred before or after deadline, setting breach flag accordingly. Idempotent against subsequent replies.
  - `recordResolution`: Computes final resolution performance, marking status as `met` or `breached`.
- **Hold Period Pause & Resume Mechanism:**
  - `pauseSla`: When ticket is placed `on_hold` (waiting for customer or third-party vendor), captures pause timestamp.
  - `resumeSla`: Computes elapsed pause interval and shifts both first-response (if pending) and resolution deadlines forward, accumulating total paused duration.
- **Breach Evaluation & Automated Escalation:**
  - `evaluateBreaches`: Scans active tickets; detects deadline breaches for both first-response and resolution.
  - Supports automated priority escalation (`low` -> `medium` -> `high` -> `critical`) to elevate urgency on overdue tickets.
