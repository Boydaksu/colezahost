## 1. Subphase Scope & Objective
Deliver comprehensive ticket assignment capabilities, automated cyclic round-robin distribution, workload-based least-loaded dispatch, unassignment workflows, and immutable assignment audit logging:
- **Assignment Strategies:**
  - `manual`: Direct assignment by supervisors/agents with validation enforcing that assignees are active members of the ticket's department.
  - `round_robin`: Department-scoped rotation pointer persisted in `support_department_assignment_state`. Dynamically queries active and assignable agents (`can_assign = 1`, `is_active = 1`), rotating deterministically and skipping non-eligible members. Safely falls back to unassigned queue if no assignable agents exist.
  - `least_loaded`: Aggregates active ticket workload (`status NOT IN ('resolved', 'closed')`) across eligible agents in the department, routing new tickets to the least busy agent.
  - `unassign`: Removes assigned agent cleanly returning the ticket to the triage queue.
- **Assignment Audit Logging:**
  - `TicketAssignmentLog` entity capturing transition records: `ticket_id`, `previous_assigned_to`, `new_assigned_to`, `assigned_by_user_id`, `strategy` (`manual`, `round_robin`, `least_loaded`, `unassign`), `reason`, and timestamp.
  - Storage table `support_ticket_assignments_log` ordered chronologically.
