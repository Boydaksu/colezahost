## 1. Subphase Scope & Objective
Establish cross-domain relations between tickets and core commerce/infrastructure entities (service, domain, invoice, order), aggregated context summaries for staff, and quick contextual actions with execution auditing:
- **Entity & Schema Relations:**
  - `Ticket` expanded with relations: `service_id`, `domain_id`, `invoice_id`, and `order_id`.
  - Schema migrations in `support_tickets` ensuring `invoice_id` and `order_id` nullable foreign keys.
  - Dynamic linking and unlinking operations (`linkResource`, `unlinkResource`) supporting all 4 resource types with validation.
  - Multi-resource ticket filtering in `listTickets`.
- **Decoupled Context Aggregation (Domain Boundary Compliant):**
  - `TicketContextProviderInterface` preventing direct database bypass into Billing/Provisioning private tables.
  - `TicketContextSummary` DTO presenting consolidated service, domain, invoice, and order details for staff sidebar.
- **Contextual Staff Actions & Audit Log:**
  - `TicketContextCommandHandlerInterface` allowing extensible command handling.
  - Built-in actions for operational triage:
    - Service: `service.sync_status`, `service.reboot`
    - Domain: `domain.sync_whois`
    - Invoice: `invoice.resend_notification`
  - Graceful validation when requested actions lack linked resources.
  - `support_context_command_logs` tracking staff actor, action, resource type/ID, success/failure outcome, and timestamp.
