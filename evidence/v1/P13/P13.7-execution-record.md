## 1. Subphase Scope & Objective
Deliver multi-tenant organization isolation, granular organizational role permissions (`owner`, `admin`, `member`), personal ticket access guards, and staff department scoping:
- **Multi-Tenant Isolation & Security Barrier:**
  - Strict cross-tenant barrier: Users belonging to Organization A cannot view, reply to, or manage any tickets belonging to Organization B, regardless of ticket ID enumeration attempts.
  - Personal tickets (`organization_id = null`) accessible only by their creator user and authorized staff.
- **Organization-Level Permissions:**
  - `org_owner` & `org_admin`: Full visibility, reply, and management rights across all tickets created under their organization (`org.tickets.all`, `org.*`).
  - `org_member`: Default visibility strictly restricted to their own tickets (`user_id === creator`), preventing internal unauthorized data leakage among colleagues.
  - Granular grant: Explicit assignment of `org.tickets.all` permits designated members to view organization-wide tickets.
- **Staff & Department Scoping:**
  - `superadmin`: Global unconstrained access across all organizations and departments.
  - `support_agent`: Requires `tickets.view` and `tickets.reply` permissions; strictly scoped to assigned departments when department memberships are defined.
- **Query & Array Filtering:**
  - `getAuthorizedQueryFilters`: Generates database-level WHERE parameters enforcing multi-tenant isolation at the SQL query stage.
  - `filterAuthorizedTickets`: Post-processing array guard preventing data leakage.
