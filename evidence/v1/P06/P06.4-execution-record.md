## 1. Components Implemented
- `Coleza\Domain\Commerce\Payments\Refund`: Refund entity recording refund sequential numbers (`REF-YYYYMMDD-XXXXXX`), payment and invoice association, user ID, amount minor, currency, structured reasons, refund methods (`manual`, `original_gateway`, `credit`), transaction reference, and timestamp.
- `Coleza\Domain\Commerce\Payments\Payment`: Enhanced with refunded amount accounting (`refundedAmountMinor`, `getRefundableAmountMinor()`) and refund status lifecycle (`partially_refunded`, `refunded`).
- `Coleza\Domain\Commerce\Payments\PaymentService`:
  - Implemented multi-transaction split payment capability where multiple independent payments incrementally reduce invoice balance due until zero, triggering order state machine activation on final full settlement.
  - Implemented refund engine (`recordRefund`) enforcing validation against refundable payment balance, updating payment state (`partially_refunded` vs `refunded`), and adjusting invoice state (`partially_paid` vs `refunded`).
  - Added query methods: `findRefundById`, `findRefundByNumber`, `listRefundsForPayment`, `listRefundsForInvoice`, and `listRefundsForUser`.
- `Coleza\Domain\Commerce\Invoices\InvoiceService`: Implemented `applyRefund` to reliably roll back paid amounts and recompute invoice status between `partially_paid`, `unpaid`, and `refunded`.
