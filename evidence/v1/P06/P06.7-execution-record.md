## 1. Components Implemented
- `Coleza\Domain\Commerce\Services\ServiceStateMachine`: Deterministic hosting service state machine enforcing states (`pending`, `active`, `suspended`, `cancelled`, `terminated`, `fraud`), valid state transitions, and guarding terminal states (`terminated` and `fraud`).
- `Coleza\Domain\Commerce\Services\Service`: Primary hosting service entity holding unique human-readable numbers (`SRV-YYYYMMDD-XXXXXX`), customer and multi-tenant scopes, order associations, product references, status, billing cycle, recurring amount, currency, registration/next-due dates, and provisioning credentials (domain, username, encrypted password, server name, IP address).
- `Coleza\Domain\Commerce\Services\ServiceService`: Lifecycle service manager providing:
  - Service creation (`createService`) with automatic next due date computation based on billing cycle.
  - Mass service provisioning from orders (`createServicesFromOrder`).
  - Provisioning activation (`activateService`) updating network credentials and transitioning state to `active`.
  - Suspension (`suspendService`) with reason auditing and unsuspension (`unsuspendService`).
  - Permanent termination (`terminateService`) and cancellation (`cancelService`).
  - Renewal progression (`renewService`) automatically advancing the next due date by cycle length and reactivating overdue services.
