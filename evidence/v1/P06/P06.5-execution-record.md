## 1. Components Implemented
- `Coleza\Domain\Commerce\Credit\CreditEntry`: Immutable ledger entity holding sequential identifiers (`CR-YYYYMMDD-XXXXXX`), customer/tenant scope (`user_id`, `organization_id`), currency code, entry type (`credit`, `debit`), amount minor, post-transaction snapshot balance (`balance_after_minor`), reason, references (`invoice`, `payment`, `refund`, `manual_adjustment`), and admin audit trail.
- `Coleza\Domain\Commerce\Credit\CreditService`: Immutable credit ledger engine providing:
  - Real-time balance evaluation (`getBalance`) partitioned strictly per user, tenant organization, and currency.
  - Credit deposit (`addCredit`) and credit deduction (`deductCredit`) with overdraft prevention.
  - Credit invoice settlement (`applyCreditToInvoice`) that deducts from credit ledger, creates an immutable payment of method `credit`, and activates orders upon full settlement.
  - Refund-to-credit workflow (`refundToCredit`) that triggers payment refund and deposits funds into the user's ledger.
  - Administrative manual adjustments (`adjustBalance`) computing delta adjustments cleanly.
  - Audit trail listing and lookup queries (`listEntriesForUser`, `findEntryById`, `findEntryByNumber`).
