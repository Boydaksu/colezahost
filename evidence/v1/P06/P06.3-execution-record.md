## 1. Components Implemented
- `Coleza\Domain\Commerce\Payments\Payment`: Core payment model managing transaction lifecycle (`pending`, `completed`, `failed`, `refunded`, `partially_refunded`), payment methods (`bank_transfer`, `manual`, `credit`), fee deduction, unallocated/allocated amount tracking, transaction references, proof documents, and allocations.
- `Coleza\Domain\Commerce\Payments\PaymentAllocation`: Relational binding linking a completed payment to an invoice (and optionally specific invoice item), tracking allocation timestamp and metadata.
- `Coleza\Domain\Commerce\Payments\PaymentService`: Payment management service providing:
  - Atomic sequence generation for payment numbers (`PAY-YYYYMMDD-XXXXXX`).
  - Direct manual cash/admin payment recording with auto-allocation.
  - Bank transfer notification workflow (starts `pending` with uploaded receipt proof).
  - Admin approval/rejection workflows (`approveManualPayment`, `rejectManualPayment`).
  - Multi-invoice manual allocation engine with strict balance check and currency consistency checks.
  - Automatic invoice balance settlement and corresponding order state transition (`OrderStateMachine::STATUS_ACTIVE`) upon invoice full payment.
- `Coleza\Domain\Commerce\Invoices\InvoiceService`: Added `applyPayment()` method to update paid amount, update balance due, and automatically mark invoices as `partially_paid` or `paid`.
