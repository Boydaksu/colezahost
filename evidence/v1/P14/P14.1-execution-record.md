## 1. Subphase Scope & Objective
Establish the foundational risk evaluation engine for Coleza Host covering order, payment, and identity dimensions:
- **Decoupled Architecture & Domain Boundary:** Adhere strictly to `02-architecture/DOMAIN_BOUNDARIES.md`: Fraud domain evaluates risk contexts and returns immutable decisions and signals without mutating or directly setting Order database fields.
- **Risk Decision Outcomes (`RiskDecision`):**
  - `ACCEPT`: Score below review threshold (default < 30).
  - `REVIEW`: Score within manual review band (default 30 <= score < 70).
  - `REJECT`: Score at or above hard reject threshold (default score >= 70).
- **Signal Aggregation & Scoring Pipeline (`RiskSignal`, `RiskContext`):**
  - `RiskSignal`: Individual rule contribution with `rule_code`, `score`, `severity`, `description`, and contextual `metadata`.
  - `RiskContext`: Rich snapshot encapsulating identity details, client IP, Tor/VPN/proxy indicators, billing country, GeoIP country, card issuing country, prepaid card status, order amount, and transaction history.
- **Modular Rule Engine (`RiskRuleInterface`):**
  - `DisposableEmailRule` (`RULE_DISPOSABLE_EMAIL`): Detects disposable temporary mailboxes (e.g. Mailinator, TempMail, GuerrillaMail).
  - `AnonymousProxyRule` (`RULE_ANONYMOUS_PROXY`): Detects VPN, commercial proxy, and Tor exit node connections.
  - `GeoIpMismatchRule` (`RULE_GEO_IP_MISMATCH`): Identifies discrepancies between customer billing country and connection geolocation.
  - `PaymentCountryMismatchRule` (`RULE_PAYMENT_COUNTRY_MISMATCH`): Flags discrepancies between credit card issuing country and billing/geo IP profile, and adds prepaid card risk penalties.
  - `HighOrderValueRule` (`RULE_HIGH_ORDER_VALUE`): Evaluates elevated order amounts against risk tiers ($500+ and $2000+).
  - `NewCustomerHighValueRule` (`RULE_NEW_CUSTOMER_HIGH_VALUE`): Penalizes high-value orders placed by first-time, unvetted customers ($150+).
- **Service & Persistence (`RiskScoreService`):**
  - Tables `fraud_risk_evaluations` and `fraud_risk_signals`.
  - Immutable JSON snapshot of evaluation context.
  - Retrieval methods by evaluation ID, entity reference (`order`, `payment`, `identity`), and user ID.
