## 1. Subphase Scope & Objective
Establish the baseline Abuse Handling and Case Management system for Coleza Host:
- **Abuse Taxonomy & Categorization (`AbuseCategory`):**
  - Canonical categories: `PHISHING`, `MALWARE`, `SPAM`, `DMCA_COPYRIGHT`, `RESOURCE_ABUSE_DDOS`, `BOTNET_C2`, `ILLEGAL_CONTENT`, and `OTHER`.
- **Severity & Dynamic SLA Deadlines (`AbuseSeverity`):**
  - `CRITICAL`: 4-hour response SLA (Emergency C2 botnet, active DDoS).
  - `HIGH`: 24-hour response SLA (Active credential harvesting, malware).
  - `MEDIUM`: 48-hour response SLA (Disputed copyright, repeat spam).
  - `LOW`: 72-hour response SLA (Minor ToS or spam report).
  - Automatic deadline computation based on severity tier with overdue detection (`isOverdue()`).
- **Resource Linkage & Multi-Tenant Scoping:**
  - Direct association with hosting resources (`service`, `domain`, `ip`, `server`, `storage`).
  - Account and organization linkage (`user_id`, `organization_id`).
  - Storage tables: `abuse_cases` and `abuse_case_messages`.
- **Communication Threading & Automatic Workflow:**
  - `AbuseMessage` with role attribution (`staff`, `client`, `reporter`, `system`) and privacy segregation (`is_internal`).
  - Automatic workflow state progression:
    - Staff message -> auto-transitions to `WAITING_CLIENT_RESPONSE`.
    - Client reply -> auto-transitions to `CLIENT_RESPONDED`.
    - Internal notes preserved without leaking to client/reporter portals.
- **Resolution, Dismissal & Emergency Quarantine:**
  - Staff resolution (`resolveCase`) with mandatory audit notes.
  - Staff dismissal (`dismissCase`) for false positives.
  - Emergency quarantine action (`suspendResourceAction`) recording an immutable staff intervention.
