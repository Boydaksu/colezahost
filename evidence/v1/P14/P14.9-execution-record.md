## 1. Subphase Scope & Objective
Establish pluggable domain/module privacy handlers and cryptographically auditable PII redaction workflows:
- **Pluggable Domain Privacy Contract (`DomainPrivacyHandlerInterface`):**
  - Modular contract for subsystems (Billing, Hosting, Domains, Support, Notifications) to participate in privacy events:
    - `getDomainName(): string`
    - `handleErasure(int $userId, string $mode = 'ANONYMIZE', array $context = []): DomainPrivacyActionResult`
    - `handleExport(int $userId): array`
    - `handleRestriction(int $userId, bool $restricted): void`
- **Domain Privacy Handlers:**
  - `BillingPrivacyHandler`: Redacts saved payment methods, credit card tokens, customer billing profiles, and company tax identifiers while preserving immutable fiscal invoice ledgers under the anonymized customer identifier.
  - `HostingPrivacyHandler`: Redacts server credentials, cPanel/server usernames, passwords, access tokens, and administrative notes on terminated hosting services.
  - `SupportPrivacyHandler`: Anonymizes support ticket authors, customer contact emails, and message bodies containing PII, and redacts IP address tracking.
- **Privacy Handler Registry & Coordinator (`PrivacyHandlerRegistry`):**
  - Coordinates cross-domain erasure execution, data export collection, and processing restrictions across all registered handlers.
- **Redaction Audit & Compliance Engine (`RedactionAuditService`):**
  - Storage table: `privacy_redaction_audits`.
  - Cryptographically signs every redaction audit record using HMAC-SHA256 (`computeSignature`, `verifySignature`).
  - Records records affected, redacted field lists, pre-redaction and post-redaction checksums, and verifying staff/system actor.
  - `scanForUnredactedPii(int $userId): RedactionScanReport`: Performs independent post-erasure compliance inspection across all sensitive domain tables (`users`, `payment_methods`, `billing_profiles`, `hosting_services`, `privacy_consents`) confirming zero residual unredacted PII remains.
- **`PrivacyErasureService` Integration:**
  - Automatically executes all registered domain handlers upon erasure.
  - Records individual domain redaction audits as well as core identity redaction audits.
  - Propagates processing restrictions to all registered handlers.
