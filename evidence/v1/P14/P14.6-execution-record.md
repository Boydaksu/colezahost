## 1. Subphase Scope & Objective
Establish statutory compliance for GDPR Article 17 (Right to Erasure) and Article 18 (Restriction of Processing) addressing fiscal retention tensions:
- **Three-Tier Privacy Strategy (`ErasureAction`):**
  - `DELETE`: Ephemeral or non-essential records (sessions, authentication tokens, marketing consents).
  - `ANONYMIZE`: User profile and identity attributes transformed into synthetic pseudonyms (`[Anonymized User #X]`, pseudonymous email, nulled personal phones/addresses/tax numbers).
  - `RETAIN`: Legally mandated financial and accounting records (invoices, tax documentation, audit logs) retained under fiscal retention laws (7-10 year preservation mandate).
- **Comprehensive Erasure Dry-Run (`ErasurePlan`, `ErasurePlanItem`):**
  - Scans user footprint across domains without mutating persistent state.
  - Eligibility evaluation: identifies critical blockers:
    - Active or suspended hosting services.
    - Active or pending domain registrations.
    - Unpaid / outstanding invoice balances.
  - Produces structured preview detailing planned counts and legal rationales.
- **Erasure Execution & Audit Trail (`ErasureExecutionResult`, `PrivacyErasureService`):**
  - Enforces prerequisite eligibility check: destructive operations are strictly blocked if blockers remain.
  - Executes anonymization and deletion workflows.
  - Immutably records erasure operation in `privacy_erasure_logs` with SHA-256 cryptographic checksum.
- **Restriction of Processing (`privacy_processing_restrictions`):**
  - Freezes user data processing during legal disputes or verification periods.
  - Supports staff un-restriction workflow with mandatory reason.
