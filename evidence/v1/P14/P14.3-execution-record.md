## 1. Subphase Scope & Objective
Establish staff manual risk review management with tamper-evident cryptographic decision auditing:
- **Decoupled Architecture & Domain Boundary:** In accordance with `02-architecture/DOMAIN_BOUNDARIES.md`: Fraud domain manages review queues and produces immutable decision snapshots without mutating or directly setting Order database fields.
- **Manual Review Queue Workflow (`RiskReviewCase`, `RiskReviewStatus`):**
  - Case lifecycles: `pending`, `in_review`, `approved`, `rejected`, `escalated`.
  - Prioritization engine: cases prioritized by urgency (`urgent` > `high` > `normal` > `low`).
  - Staff assignment: claiming, assigning, and escalating cases.
  - Idempotent review case creation preventing duplicates for identical evaluations.
  - Storage table `fraud_risk_reviews`.
- **Immutable Decision Snapshots (`RiskReviewDecisionSnapshot`):**
  - Records manual resolution: `original_decision`, `final_decision`, `is_override`, `override_reason`, `decided_by_staff_id`, `decided_at`, and `metadata`.
  - Strict override reasoning: mandatory non-empty reason when approving flagged orders or rejecting acceptable orders.
  - Resolved case immutability: once approved or rejected, review case cannot be mutated or re-resolved.
  - Storage table `fraud_risk_review_decisions`.
- **Cryptographic Tamper-Evidence:**
  - SHA-256 canonical hashing across decision parameters (`calculateChecksum`, `verifyIntegrity`).
  - Detects any subsequent modification to reason, final decision, staff attribution, or timestamp.
