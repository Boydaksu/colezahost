## 1. Subphase Scope & Objective
Establish automated data retention schedules and legal hold preservation controls:
- **Data Retention Policies Engine (`RetentionPolicy`):**
  - Configurable and statutory data retention timelines:
    - `financial_documents`: 2,555 days (~7 years), statutory tax/accounting compliance, action on expiry: archive.
    - `audit_logs`: 365 days (1 year), statutory compliance, action: anonymize.
    - `support_tickets`: 730 days (2 years), action: anonymize.
    - `security_events`: 180 days, action: anonymize.
    - `ephemeral_sessions`: 30 days, action: delete.
  - Lifecycle expiration evaluation: `isExpired(recordDate, now)` and `getExpirationDate(recordDate)`.
  - Storage table: `privacy_retention_policies`.
- **Legal Hold (Litigation Hold) Preservation (`LegalHold`, `LegalHoldScopeType`):**
  - Scope types: `USER`, `ORGANIZATION`, `RESOURCE`, and `GLOBAL`.
  - Formal authority citation, reference tracking (`hold_reference`), staff creator, and active state.
  - Life cycle: `placeLegalHold` and `releaseLegalHold` with mandatory release justification.
  - Storage table: `privacy_legal_holds`.
- **Statutory Cross-System Enforcement:**
  - `isSubjectUnderLegalHold`: Real-time query checking active holds across user, organization, or resource identifier.
  - `assertCanPurgeOrErase`: Throws `ValidationException` blocking data purging or destruction for subjects under hold.
  - `PrivacyErasureService` integration: Integrates with legal hold engine in `executeDryRun`; an active legal hold immediately registers as a hard blocker preventing account erasure.
