## 1. Subphase Scope & Objective
Establish specialized, role-based dashboard view models and aggregation services across the 5 primary business verticals:
- **Executive / Owner Dashboard (`OwnerDashboardData`):**
  - High-level business health summary: Total MRR, Annualized Run Rate (ARR), trailing 30-day net cash flow, net MRR growth, active subscriptions count, active customer count, ARPU, and open accounts receivable.
  - Multi-period historical MRR trend visualization data.
- **Finance Dashboard (`FinanceDashboardData`):**
  - Accounting billed claims vs actual captured cash liquidity.
  - Estimated sales tax / VAT liability obligations.
  - Aged receivables debt schedule (current, 30d, 60d, 90+d overdue buckets).
  - Payment gateway fees summary and net contribution margin percentage.
- **Sales / Growth Dashboard (`SalesDashboardData`):**
  - New orders count and new gross order volume.
  - New customer acquisition metrics.
  - Incremental New MRR added from new contracts.
  - Top-selling hosting packages and product breakdown.
- **Operations & Infrastructure Dashboard (`OperationsDashboardData`):**
  - Active, suspended, and terminated hosting services distribution.
  - Domains under management and upcoming 30-day domain renewal expirations.
  - Open abuse case count and hypervisor capacity utilization.
- **Support & Service Desk Dashboard (`SupportDashboardData`):**
  - Tickets opened vs resolved in period.
  - Current open tickets backlog.
  - Average first response time (minutes).
  - SLA compliance rate percentage.
  - Departmental and ticket priority volume distributions.
- **Unified Compilation Service (`DashboardService`):**
  - Reusable application service providing typed, JSON-serializable dashboard payloads for UI frontend and API consumers.
