## 1. Subphase Scope & Objective
Establish subscription economics, MRR/ARR normalization algorithms, subscription movement categorization, and point-in-time snapshot persistence:
- **Normalized MRR & ARR Mechanics (`MrrCalculator`):**
  - Formula: Normalizes multi-period billing cycles into exact monthly values (`monthly` = 1.0, `quarterly` = 1/3, `semi_annually` = 1/6, `annually` = 1/12, `biennially` = 1/24, `triennially` = 1/36).
  - Explicit handling of non-recurring plans (`one_time`, `free` = 0.0 MRR).
  - Annual Run Rate derivation: `ARR = MRR * 12`.
- **MRR Movement Bridge & Waterfall (`MrrMovementType`, `MrrWaterfallReport`):**
  - Canonical subscription movements: `NEW`, `EXPANSION`, `CONTRACTION`, `CHURN`, and `REACTIVATION`.
  - Waterfall balance equation: `Ending MRR = Starting MRR + New + Expansion - Contraction - Churn + Reactivation`.
  - Calculates net growth amount and percentage growth velocity across arbitrary date intervals.
- **Point-in-Time Subscription Snapshots (`SubscriptionSnapshot`, `SubscriptionSnapshotService`):**
  - Persistence table: `analytics_subscription_snapshots`.
  - Evaluates active contracts in `hosting_services`, aggregating active subscription count, unique customer count, total MRR, total ARR, and Average Revenue Per User (ARPU).
  - Calculates delta changes compared to previous historical snapshots to classify new MRR and churned MRR.
  - Generates audit event `ANALYTICS_SUBSCRIPTION_SNAPSHOT_CAPTURED`.
  - Enforces strict date formatting and currency-isolated aggregation.
