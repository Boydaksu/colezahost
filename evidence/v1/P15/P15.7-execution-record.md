## 1. Subphase Scope & Objective
Implement deterministic golden financial fixtures and source-ledger reconciliation services enforcing the core architectural invariant:
**Source-Ledger Reconciliation = Zero Unexplained Diff.**

- **Golden Financial Dataset Fixture (`GoldenFinancialDataset`):**
  - Canonical dataset establishing deterministic financial reality:
    - Invoices: Paid, unpaid/AR, and cancelled.
    - Payments: Gateway captured/completed transactions.
    - Refunds: Partial and full refunds.
    - Finance authoritative double-entry account ledger: `financial_account_transactions` credits (payments) and debits (refunds).
    - Analytics precomputed daily read models: `analytics_daily_aggregations`.
  - Deterministic totals:
    - Gross invoiced: $1,250.00, Net invoiced: $1,100.00, Tax billed: $150.00.
    - Cash collected: $950.00.
    - Refunds issued: $100.00.
    - Net cash flow: $850.00.
    - Ledger credit minor: 95,000 cents ($950.00).
    - Ledger debit minor: 10,000 cents ($100.00).
    - Ledger net minor: 85,000 cents ($850.00).
  - Anomaly injection methods to test discrepancy detection (`injectLedgerDiscrepancy`, `injectAnalyticsDiscrepancy`).
- **Ledger Reconciliation Service (`LedgerReconciliationService`, `LedgerReconciliationReport`, `ReconciliationStatus`):**
  - Compares analytics read-model aggregation figures or `FinancialPeriodSummary` against authoritative double-entry ledger transactions (`financial_account_transactions`).
  - Strict zero-diff verification:
    - Payment credits variance: `analytics_cash_collected - ledger_payments`.
    - Refund debits variance: `analytics_cash_refunded - ledger_refunds`.
    - Net cash movement variance: `analytics_net_cash - ledger_net_cash`.
  - Flags status as `MATCHED` only when `abs(variance) < 0.001` with zero unexplained difference.
  - Generates itemized discrepancy descriptions when differences occur.
