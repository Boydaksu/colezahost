## 1. Subphase Scope & Objective
Establish CQRS pre-aggregated read models and historical rebuild/backfill infrastructure:
- **Core Architecture Rule (`02-architecture/SYSTEM_ARCHITECTURE.md`):**
  - "Business state source-of-truth lives in domain persistence; cache/analytics are derived."
  - "Trusted dashboards without making analytics source-of-truth."
- **Pre-Aggregated Read Models:**
  - `analytics_daily_aggregations`: Pre-computes daily rolls of invoiced volume, net claims, tax billed, settled cash, refunds, net cash flow, gateway processing fees, new orders, new customer registrations, and support ticket volumes.
  - `analytics_monthly_aggregations`: Pre-computes monthly rolls along with end-of-month MRR/ARR and subscription changes.
- **Derived State Rebuild Engine (`ReadModelAggregationService`):**
  - Point-in-time calculation and idempotent upsert (`aggregateDay`, `aggregateMonth`).
  - Historical date range backfill/rebuild (`rebuildDateRange`, `rebuildMonthRange`) generating signed `RebuildExecutionReport` summaries with execution durations and SHA-256 integrity checksums.
  - Audit logging of rebuild actions: `ANALYTICS_READ_MODELS_REBUILT`.
- **High-Performance Query Interface:**
  - `getDailySeries`: Retrieves indexed chronological daily data for charts without executing expensive table scans across transactional tables.
  - `getMonthlySeries`: Delivers fast month-over-month trends for executive dashboards.
