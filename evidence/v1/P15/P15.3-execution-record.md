## 1. Subphase Scope & Objective
Establish core financial performance analytics enforcing the strict business distinction between Billed Invoices, Settled Cash, and Aged Receivables:
- **Foundational Architecture Principles (`00-overview/DECISION_REGISTER.md`):**
  - "Analytics Metric Registry/Semantic Layer; revenue/cash/invoice ayrımı."
  - "Analytics cannot be consulted as authoritative billing/payment state."
- **Invoice vs Cash Metrics Engine (`FinancialMetricsService`):**
  - **Invoice Accounting Claims:**
    - `grossInvoiced`: Sum of all non-cancelled invoice amounts issued in period.
    - `netInvoiced`: Billed subtotal before taxes.
    - `taxBilled`: Total tax/VAT recorded on invoices.
    - Invoices counts: total issued, paid count, unpaid count.
  - **Real Liquidity Settlements:**
    - `grossCashCollected`: Real monetary funds captured across payment gateways.
    - `cashRefunded`: Funds refunded to clients via gateways.
    - `netCashFlow`: `grossCashCollected - cashRefunded`.
  - **Collection Efficiency:** Ratio of cash collected to gross invoiced volume.
- **Aged Receivables & Overdue Debt Bucketing (`AgingReceivablesReport`):**
  - Evaluates open unpaid balances on invoices past their due dates.
  - Granular aging classification buckets:
    - Current (0–30 days overdue).
    - 31–60 days overdue.
    - 61–90 days overdue.
    - 91+ days overdue.
- **Unit Economics & Contribution Margin (`ContributionMarginReport`):**
  - Captures payment gateway processing fees (`fee_amount` on captured payments).
  - Integrates direct infrastructure and upstream costs.
  - Computes `netContribution = netCashFlow - gatewayFees - directCosts` and Contribution Margin percentage.
